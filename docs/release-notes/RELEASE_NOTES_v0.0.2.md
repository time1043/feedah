# v0.0.2

Review sessions now remember where you left off, the words list follows the
heatmap day, and reinstalling no longer scrambles your daily wrap-ups.

## Review picks up where you left off

- Leave a review halfway — flagged words, a past round, or a day's list —
  and the next time you step in, you start at the furthest card you swiped
  to, not from the top.
- Swiping back never loses your place, and finishing a review clears it so
  the next pass starts fresh.
- Resuming no longer lands stuck between two cards.

## The words list follows your day

- The words list now mirrors the stats heatmap: when the selected day
  changes, the list opens right at that day's first learned word —
  switching the list for you if the day's words live in another one.
- Coming back to stats always shows today again, and the words list
  follows along. While the day stays the same, browsing the list is never
  disturbed.
- A day without learning anchors the list at the very first word.

## Meanings on your terms

- The words list hides the Chinese meanings by default — tap the eye icon
  next to the word count to show or hide them. Your choice is remembered.

## Fixes

- Reinstalling the app no longer breaks the day review — a day's words now
  rebuild correctly from your synced progress instead of listing everything
  from the very first word.
